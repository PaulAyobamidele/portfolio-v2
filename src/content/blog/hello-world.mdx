# Hello World

Welcome to my blog! I'm Ayobamidele — a fullstack AI engineer and researcher based in Montreal, Canada.

## Why This Blog?

I'm a man of many interests, and they don't always fit neatly into one box. This blog is my space to explore all of them — openly, honestly, and on my own terms.

## What to Expect

**Research Pursuits** — I'll be sharing accepted papers and summaries of what I've been exploring. Think of it as a window into what's keeping me up at night.

**Technology** — My honest thoughts on the tech that catches my eye — what excites me, what doesn't, and why.

**Philosophy** — New ideas I'm learning, reflections on God, and the journey that ties it all together.

**Other Research Fields** — I'm endlessly curious. I'll be sharing discoveries from fields beyond my own and what I'm learning along the way.

## Let's Connect

If something resonates with you, reach out! I'm always happy to discuss ideas, collaborate on projects, or just chat about tech.

Stay curious.
